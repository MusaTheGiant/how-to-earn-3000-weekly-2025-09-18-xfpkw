📋 Terms, Conditions, Rules & Exceptions

Every system has rules. Here are ours, explained clearly and honestly.

How Points Are Counted

a) We count all new points from both legs (to infinity) each week. You get credit for all activity under you, regardless of depth. Each purchased seat counts for one point. If you enroll a Miner who buys a 3-pack, that counts as three points.

b) We stop counting at 9. Even if your team brings in 400 new points in a week, we cap the count at 9 per leg. This protects the sustainability of our system and ensures long-term viability for everyone.

c) Unpaid points roll over forever. If you earn 7 points this week but don't qualify, those points don't disappear. They roll over until they're paid. You never lose points unless they're cashed out.

"This isn't a race. It's a marathon. We've built Rapid Rewards to reward consistency, not just explosive bursts. Your effort compounds over time, and we protect that growth."

👥 Building Your Team: Strategy & Support

Every Miner who wants to participate in Rapid Rewards is responsible for understanding the rules and building both legs. However, TexitCoin encourages strategic cooperation.

The Power Leg & Pay Leg System

If your Sponsor is actively building one leg (we call it your Power Leg), you may only need to build the other side (your Pay Leg) to qualify for commissions. If both of you build consistently, you win faster, together.

Team-building strategies like one-leg stacking are encouraged when both parties are serious. This helps your downline earn quicker, making duplication easy and rewarding.
